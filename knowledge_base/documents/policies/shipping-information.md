## Order Processing

- Orders placed before **2 PM EST** on business days are typically processed the same day.
- Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next business day.
- You will receive a **shipping confirmation email** with tracking information once your order ships.

## Live Plant Shipping

We take special care when shipping live plants to ensure they arrive healthy and vibrant.

### How We Ship Plants
- Plants are shipped in **custom-designed, ventilated boxes** with secure root ball wrapping.
- Pots are stabilized to prevent tipping and soil spillage.
- Heat packs are included for **cold weather shipping** (when temperatures at origin or destination are below 40°F / 4°C).
- Cool packs are included during **extreme summer heat** (above 95°F / 35°C).

### Live Plant Shipping Schedule
- **Live plants ship Monday through Wednesday only** to avoid weekend delays at carrier facilities.
- Orders containing live plants placed after Wednesday at 2 PM EST will ship the following Monday.

### Upon Delivery
- **Open packages immediately.** Plants have been in a dark box and need light and air.
- Water thoroughly upon arrival.
- Allow 1–2 weeks for plants to acclimate to their new environment before judging their condition.
- If your plant arrives damaged, refer to our **Plant Guarantee Policy** for claims.

## Shipping Restrictions

- We currently ship within the **contiguous United States (48 states).**
- **Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. territories:** Limited availability. Additional shipping charges may apply. Live plants cannot be shipped to these locations.
- **International shipping** is not available at this time.
- Some products (e.g., certain fertilizers, chemicals) may have shipping restrictions to specific states due to regulations.

## Large & Oversized Items

- Items such as raised bed kits, large planters, and power tools may ship via freight carrier.
- **Freight delivery** is to curbside. Inside delivery is available for an additional fee.
- You will be contacted by the carrier to schedule a delivery window.

## Tracking Your Order

- Track your order anytime at **greenthumb.com/track** or via the link in your shipping confirmation email.
- For any shipping issues, contact us at **shipping@greenthumb.com** or call **1-800-555-GROW**.

> **Note:** Delivery times may be affected by severe weather, carrier delays, or peak season volume (especially during spring planting season, March–May).
