# GreenThumb Warranty Policy for Garden Tools & Equipment

*Last updated: January 2024*

## Overview

GreenThumb stands behind the quality of every tool and piece of equipment we sell. Our warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use conditions.

## Warranty Coverage by Category

### Hand Tools (Pruners, Trowels, Rakes, Shovels, Hoes, Weeders)
- **Warranty period:** 5 years from date of purchase.
- **Coverage:** Defects in materials or workmanship, including blade breakage, handle failure, and spring mechanism defects under normal use.
- **Not covered:** Damage from misuse, neglect, modification, or normal wear and tear (blade dulling, cosmetic scratches).

### Cutting Tools (Loppers, Pruning Saws, Hedge Shears)
- **Warranty period:** 3 years from date of purchase.
- **Coverage:** Blade defects, pivot mechanism failure, handle breakage under normal use.
- **Not covered:** Damage from cutting materials beyond the tool's rated capacity, rust from improper storage.

### Power Tools (Electric/Battery Mowers, Trimmers, Tillers, Blowers)
- **Warranty period:** 2 years from date of purchase.
- **Coverage:** Motor defects, battery failure (for battery-powered tools), manufacturing defects in housing or mechanical components.
- **Not covered:** Damage from improper use, unauthorized repairs, lack of maintenance (e.g., failure to clean air filters), normal consumable wear (blades, trimmer line, filters).
- **Battery warranty:** Lithium-ion batteries are covered for 2 years or 500 charge cycles, whichever comes first.

### Irrigation Equipment (Timers, Drip Kits, Sprinklers, Hoses)
- **Warranty period:** 1 year from date of purchase.
- **Coverage:** Manufacturing defects, premature failure under normal use conditions.
- **Not covered:** Freeze damage, clogging from sediment or hard water, damage from improper installation.

### Raised Beds, Planters & Structures
- **Warranty period:** 3 years for structural integrity.
- **Coverage:** Warping, splitting, or structural failure under normal use.
- **Not covered:** Natural weathering, color fading, cosmetic changes inherent to natural materials (e.g., cedar graying).

## How to File a Warranty Claim

1. **Contact us** at **warranty@greenthumb.com** or call **1-800-555-GROW**.
2. Provide your **order number** and **date of purchase.**
3. Describe the defect and include **clear photos** of the issue.
4. Our team will review your claim within **3–5 business days.**
5. If approved, we will:
   - **Replace** the defective item (preferred), or
   - **Repair** the item at no charge, or
   - Issue a **store credit or refund** if replacement is unavailable.

## Important Conditions

- Warranty is **non-transferable** and applies only to the original purchaser.
- Proof of purchase is required. We can look up orders placed on greenthumb.com.
- Warranty does not cover **commercial or professional use** unless the product is specifically rated for commercial applications.
- Replacement parts (blades, handles, springs) are available for purchase on our website even outside the warranty period.
- Warranty claims for **freight-shipped items** may require photos for assessment before a return is authorized to avoid unnecessary shipping costs.

> **Tip:** Register your tools at **greenthumb.com/register** within 30 days of purchase to streamline future warranty claims and receive maintenance reminders.
