# GreenThumb Return and Refund Policy

*Last updated: January 2024*

## Our Commitment

At GreenThumb, we want you to be completely satisfied with every purchase. If something isn't right, we're here to help. Please review the following policy for details on returns, exchanges, and refunds.

## General Return Policy

- **Return window:** 30 days from the date of delivery.
- **Condition:** Items must be unused, in original packaging, and in resalable condition.
- **Proof of purchase:** Order confirmation email or order number required for all returns.

## Return Eligibility by Product Category

### Garden Tools & Equipment
- Eligible for return within 30 days if unused and in original packaging.
- Defective tools may be returned at any time within the warranty period (see our Warranty Policy for Tools).
- Power tools must be returned in their original box with all accessories included.

### Seeds
- **Unopened seed packets:** Eligible for return within 30 days.
- **Opened seed packets:** Not eligible for return due to the nature of the product.
- If seeds fail to germinate under proper conditions, please contact our support team — we offer a **germination guarantee** on all GreenThumb-branded seeds.

### Live Plants
- Due to their perishable nature, live plants follow a separate policy (see our Plant Guarantee Policy).
- **Damaged in transit:** Report within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will ship a replacement or issue a full refund.
- **Plants that arrive healthy but later decline:** Not eligible for return, but our support team can help troubleshoot care issues.

### Soil, Mulch & Fertilizer
- **Unopened bags:** Eligible for return within 30 days.
- **Opened bags:** Not eligible for return.

### Pots, Planters & Accessories
- Eligible for return within 30 days if unused and undamaged.
- Custom or personalized items cannot be returned.

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Log into your account at **greenthumb.com/orders**.
2. Select the order and items you wish to return.
3. Choose your reason for return.
4. Print the prepaid return shipping label (for eligible items).
5. Package items securely and drop off at the indicated carrier.

## Refunds

- Refunds are processed within **5–7 business days** after we receive and inspect the returned item.
- Refunds are issued to the **original payment method.**
- Original shipping charges are **non-refundable** unless the return is due to our error or a defective product.
- **Restocking fee:** A 15% restocking fee may apply to large or heavy items (power tools, bulk soil orders).

## Exchanges

- We offer **free exchanges** for different sizes, colors, or varieties within 30 days.
- Subject to availability. If the requested item is out of stock, a full refund will be issued.

## Non-Returnable Items

- Opened seeds and live plants (see specific policies above)
- Gift cards
- Downloadable content and digital products
- Items marked as "Final Sale"
- Custom or personalized orders
