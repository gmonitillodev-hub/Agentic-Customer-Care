# GreenThumb Live Plant Health Guarantee

*Last updated: January 2024*

## Our Promise

We take tremendous pride in the quality of every plant we ship. All live plants from GreenThumb are inspected, carefully packaged, and shipped to arrive in excellent health. If your plant doesn't meet your expectations upon arrival, we'll make it right.

## What's Covered

### Transit Damage Guarantee
If your plant arrives **damaged, wilted, or dead** due to shipping, we will provide a **free replacement or full refund** — your choice.

**Requirements:**
- Report the issue within **48 hours** of delivery.
- Provide **clear photographs** of:
  - The plant showing the damage
  - The packaging (to help us improve our shipping process)
- Contact us at **plantcare@greenthumb.com** or call **1-800-555-GROW**.

### 30-Day Health Guarantee
If your plant shows signs of **disease, pest infestation, or failure to thrive** within **30 days of delivery** despite following our care instructions, we will issue a **one-time replacement or store credit**.

**Requirements:**
- Describe the care you've provided (light, water, soil, location).
- Provide photos of the plant and its growing conditions.
- Our horticulture team will review your claim and may offer care advice before issuing a replacement.

## What's NOT Covered

- **Neglect or improper care:** Plants that decline due to overwatering, underwatering, incorrect light, extreme temperatures, or failure to follow provided care instructions.
- **Environmental damage:** Frost damage after delivery, pet damage, accidental breakage, or exposure to herbicides.
- **Transplant shock beyond 30 days:** Some plants experience temporary shock after transplanting. This is normal and typically resolves within 2–4 weeks.
- **Natural dormancy:** Some plants go dormant seasonally (e.g., bulbs, deciduous trees). Dormancy is not a defect.
- **Cosmetic issues:** Minor leaf yellowing, a few dropped leaves during transit, or slight wilting that recovers within 48 hours with proper watering and light.

## How to File a Claim

1. **Email** plantcare@greenthumb.com with:
   - Your order number
   - Photos of the plant and packaging
   - Description of the issue and care provided
2. Our team will respond within **24–48 hours** with a resolution.
3. **No need to return the plant** — we trust our customers and do not require you to ship live plants back to us.

## Important Notes

- The guarantee applies to **one replacement per plant per order.**
- Replacement plants are subject to availability. If your original variety is out of stock, we'll offer a comparable substitute or store credit.
- The guarantee covers only plants purchased directly from **greenthumb.com** — plants purchased through third-party retailers should be returned to the retailer.
- Bulk and wholesale orders may have different guarantee terms as specified in the wholesale agreement.

## Tips for Healthy Delivery

- **Track your package** and be available to receive it promptly.
- **Open immediately** upon delivery — don't leave the box in the sun, cold, or a hot car.
- **Water immediately** and place in appropriate light conditions.
- **Allow 1–2 weeks** for acclimation before repotting or fertilizing.

> **Tip:** Each plant ships with a **care card** specific to that species. Keep it as a reference — or visit greenthumb.com/care-guides for our full digital library.
