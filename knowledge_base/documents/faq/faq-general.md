# GreenThumb — Frequently Asked Questions

*Last updated: January 2024*

## Account & Registration

### Do I need an account to place an order?
No, you can check out as a guest. However, creating a free account lets you track orders, save addresses, view order history, earn rewards points, and manage returns more easily.

### How do I create an account?
Click "Sign Up" on greenthumb.com and enter your name, email, and a password. You can also sign up with your Google or Apple account for one-click registration.

### I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. We'll send a reset link to your registered email. The link expires in 24 hours for security.

## Orders

### How do I check my order status?
Log into your account and visit "My Orders," or use the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email. Guest orders can be tracked at **greenthumb.com/track** using your order number and email address.

### Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?
- **Within 1 hour** of ordering: You can modify or cancel directly from your account.
- **After 1 hour:** Contact customer support as soon as possible. We process orders quickly, so changes cannot be guaranteed once an order enters fulfillment.

### What if an item is out of stock?
Out-of-stock items display an "Email Me When Available" option. You'll receive an automatic notification when the item is restocked. We never charge for out-of-stock items.

## Payment

### What payment methods do you accept?
- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- PayPal and PayPal Credit
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- GreenThumb Gift Cards
- Affirm (buy now, pay later — available on orders over $50)

### Is my payment information secure?
Yes. All transactions are encrypted with 256-bit SSL. We are PCI-DSS compliant and never store full credit card numbers on our servers.

### Do you charge sales tax?
Sales tax is calculated and charged based on the shipping destination, in accordance with state and local tax laws.

## Gift Cards

### How do gift cards work?
GreenThumb digital gift cards are delivered by email and can be used on any product on our website. They never expire and have no fees.

### Can I use multiple gift cards on one order?
Yes, you can combine up to 3 gift cards per order. Any remaining balance stays on the card for future use.

### Can gift cards be refunded?
Gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash, except where required by law.

## Rewards Program

### How does the GreenThumb Rewards program work?
- Earn **1 point per $1 spent.**
- **100 points = $5 reward** applied to your next order.
- Bonus points are available during seasonal promotions and for product reviews.
- Points expire after **12 months** of account inactivity.
