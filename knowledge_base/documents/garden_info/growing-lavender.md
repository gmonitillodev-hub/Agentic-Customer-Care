# Complete Guide to Growing and Maintaining Lavender

## Introduction

Lavender is a gardener's dream — fragrant, beautiful, drought-tolerant, and loved by pollinators. Whether you grow it for its scent, culinary uses, or ornamental beauty, lavender is surprisingly easy to cultivate once you understand its preferences.

## Popular Varieties

- **English Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia):** Most cold-hardy (Zones 5–8). Best for culinary use. Varieties: 'Hidcote,' 'Munstead.'
- **French Lavender (Lavandula dentata):** Mild climates (Zones 8–11). Distinctive serrated leaves.
- **Spanish Lavender (Lavandula stoechas):** Unique pineapple-shaped flowers. Zones 7–10.
- **Lavandin (Lavandula x intermedia):** Hybrid with high oil content. Excellent for sachets and crafts.

## Growing Conditions

- **Sun:** Full sun — minimum 6–8 hours of direct sunlight daily.
- **Soil:** Well-drained, slightly alkaline (pH 6.5–7.5). Sandy or gravelly soil is ideal.
- **Water:** Drought-tolerant once established. Overwatering is the number one killer of lavender.
- **Spacing:** 12–18 inches apart to ensure good airflow.

## Planting Tips

1. Choose a location with excellent drainage — lavender will not survive in soggy soil.
2. If your soil is heavy clay, amend with coarse sand and gravel or plant in raised beds.
3. Do **not** add rich compost — lavender thrives in lean, poor soil.
4. Water newly planted lavender weekly until established (first growing season).

## Pruning & Maintenance

- **Prune annually** in early spring or after the first flush of blooms fades.
- Cut back about one-third of the plant — never cut into old, woody growth (it won't regrow).
- Remove spent flower stems to encourage a second bloom in late summer.
- Shape into a mound to prevent the center from splitting open.

## Harvesting

- Harvest when about **half the flower buds** on each spike have opened.
- Cut stems long — they're perfect for drying in small bundles hung upside down.

> **Tip:** Lavender is deer-resistant and repels mosquitoes, making it an excellent companion plant around patios and seating areas.
