# Spring Bulb Planting Guide: Tulips, Daffodils & Crocus

## Introduction

Few things announce spring more beautifully than beds of tulips, daffodils, and crocus bursting into bloom. The secret? These bulbs need to be planted in autumn to provide the cold period they require for flowering.

## When to Plant

- **Ideal window:** September through November, before the ground freezes.
- **Soil temperature:** Below 60°F (15°C) at a 6-inch depth.
- Plant crocus and daffodils first (early fall), tulips last (mid to late fall).

## Choosing Healthy Bulbs

- Select bulbs that are **firm and plump** — avoid soft, moldy, or damaged ones.
- Larger bulbs generally produce bigger, more vigorous blooms.
- Buy from reputable suppliers for disease-free stock.

## Planting Depth & Spacing

| Bulb Type  | Planting Depth | Spacing   |
|------------|---------------|-----------|
| Tulips     | 6–8 inches    | 4–6 inches |
| Daffodils  | 6–8 inches    | 6–8 inches |
| Crocus     | 3–4 inches    | 2–3 inches |

> **Rule of thumb:** Plant bulbs at a depth roughly 2–3 times their height, pointed end facing up.

## Step-by-Step Planting

1. **Prepare the soil** — loosen to 12 inches deep and mix in compost for drainage.
2. **Dig holes or trenches** to the appropriate depth.
3. **Place bulbs** pointed side up. If unsure, plant sideways — they'll find their way.
4. **Backfill with soil** and press gently.
5. **Water thoroughly** after planting to settle the soil and initiate root growth.
6. **Apply mulch** (2–3 inches) to insulate against temperature fluctuations.

## Care Tips

- **Do not cut foliage** after blooming — let it yellow and die back naturally so bulbs can store energy.
- **Fertilize** in early spring with a balanced bulb fertilizer.
- **Protect from squirrels** by laying wire mesh over planting areas.

> **Warning:** All parts of daffodil bulbs are toxic. Wear gloves when handling and keep away from pets.
