# How to Choose and Use Organic Fertilizers

## Introduction

Organic fertilizers feed your soil as well as your plants. Unlike synthetic fertilizers that deliver a quick chemical boost, organic options release nutrients slowly as they're broken down by soil microorganisms — building long-term soil health in the process.

## Understanding NPK

All fertilizers display three numbers representing their **N-P-K ratio:**

- **N (Nitrogen):** Promotes leafy green growth.
- **P (Phosphorus):** Supports root development and flowering.
- **K (Potassium):** Strengthens overall plant health and disease resistance.

## Common Organic Fertilizers

### Animal-Based
- **Blood meal (12-0-0):** Fast-acting nitrogen source. Great for leafy vegetables. Use sparingly — can burn plants.
- **Bone meal (3-15-0):** Excellent phosphorus source for root growth and flowering. Ideal for bulbs and transplants.
- **Fish emulsion (5-1-1):** Liquid nitrogen boost. Smells unpleasant but extremely effective. Dilute before applying.
- **Worm castings (1-0-0):** Gentle, balanced nutrition. Cannot burn plants. Perfect for seedlings and containers.

### Plant-Based
- **Alfalfa meal (2.5-1-1):** Slow-release nitrogen, also contains triacontanol (a growth stimulant).
- **Kelp meal (1-0-2):** Rich in micronutrients and growth hormones. Excellent soil conditioner.
- **Soybean meal (7-2-1):** Good nitrogen source, slow-release.

### Mineral-Based
- **Greensand (0-0-3):** Slow-release potassium and trace minerals. Improves clay soil.
- **Rock phosphate (0-3-0):** Long-lasting phosphorus source. Takes months to become available.

## Application Guidelines

- **Timing:** Apply in spring before planting and mid-season as a side dressing.
- **Method:** Work granular fertilizers into the top 2–3 inches of soil. Apply liquid fertilizers as a diluted drench or foliar spray.
- **Don't overdo it:** More is not better. Over-fertilizing with organics is harder to do than with synthetics, but excess nitrogen still causes leggy growth and poor fruiting.

## Making Your Own

A simple all-purpose organic blend:
- 4 parts seed meal (alfalfa or soybean)
- 1 part bone meal
- 1 part kelp meal

Mix thoroughly and apply 4–6 cups per 100 square feet of garden bed.

> **Tip:** Organic fertilizers work best in biologically active soil. Always combine fertilization with regular compost additions to support the soil microbiome.
