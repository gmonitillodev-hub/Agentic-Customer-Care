# Month-by-Month Planting Calendar

## Introduction

Timing is everything in gardening. This month-by-month calendar provides a general guide for temperate climates (USDA Zones 5–8). Adjust dates based on your specific zone and local frost dates.

## January – February

- **Indoors:** Order seeds and supplies. Start slow-growing seeds like onions, leeks, and celery under grow lights.
- **Outdoors:** Plan garden layout. Prune dormant fruit trees and ornamental trees (avoid spring-flowering types).
- **Maintenance:** Clean and organize tools. Review last year's garden journal.

## March

- **Indoors:** Start tomatoes, peppers, eggplant, and herbs 6–8 weeks before last frost.
- **Outdoors (if soil is workable):** Direct sow peas, spinach, and fava beans. Plant bare-root roses, trees, and berry bushes.
- **Maintenance:** Turn compost pile. Prepare raised beds with fresh compost.

## April

- **Indoors:** Start cucumbers, squash, and melons 3–4 weeks before transplanting.
- **Outdoors:** Sow lettuce, radishes, carrots, beets, and kale. Plant seed potatoes. Transplant cool-season seedlings. Divide perennials.
- **Maintenance:** Apply pre-emergent weed control to lawns. Set up irrigation.

## May

- **After last frost:** Transplant tomatoes, peppers, and herbs outdoors. Direct sow beans, corn, sunflowers, and zinnias.
- **Plant:** Summer-blooming bulbs (dahlias, gladiolus, lilies). Hanging baskets and container gardens.
- **Maintenance:** Mulch all beds. Install supports for climbing plants.

## June – July

- **Succession sow:** Beans, lettuce, carrots, and beets every 2–3 weeks for continuous harvest.
- **Plant:** Fall-harvest crops in late July (broccoli, cabbage, Brussels sprouts starts).
- **Maintenance:** Water consistently. Harvest regularly to encourage production. Watch for pests.

## August – September

- **Plant:** Fall crops — kale, spinach, radishes, lettuce, and turnips. Plant fall-blooming perennials.
- **Start planning:** Order spring-blooming bulbs and garlic for fall planting.
- **Maintenance:** Begin fall garden cleanup as summer crops finish.

## October – November

- **Plant:** Garlic, spring-blooming bulbs (tulips, daffodils, crocus). Transplant trees and shrubs.
- **Sow cover crops** in empty beds. Collect and dry seeds from the garden.
- **Maintenance:** Rake leaves for compost. Winterize irrigation systems. Protect tender plants.

## December

- **Indoors:** Force bulbs (amaryllis, paperwhites) for holiday blooms. Plan next year's garden.
- **Outdoors:** Apply winter mulch. Protect container plants from freezing.
- **Maintenance:** Clean and store tools. Review the year and update your garden journal.

> **Note:** This calendar is a general guide. Always check your local frost dates and adjust accordingly.
